# Defensive Reorganization Context v1 — IDSSE result

**Formal status:** **DEFENSIVE REORGANIZATION CONTEXT v1 SUPPORTED**

This governed descriptive study uses observed near-minus-middle defender-relative path, not DRD or residuals. Its two pre-movement contexts are attacker goalward position relative to the defensive unit and attacker–ball distance. The seven IDSSE matches contributed 64,805 eligible attacker-anchor observations; every match retained both periods and the frozen full-rank model fit.

## Pooled associations

| Context | Estimate (m/m) | 97.5% interval | Individual support gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `attacker_minus_unit_goalward_m` | -0.010161 | [-0.011805, -0.008499] | PASS |
| `attacker_ball_distance_start_m` | -0.007533 | [-0.008864, -0.006245] | PASS |

Both contexts had the pooled negative direction in all seven match-specific
fits and all seven leave-one-match-out fits. The frozen joint central-support
trim retained 58,624 observations (90.46%): its H1 and H2 estimates were
-0.010136 and -0.008148 m/m, respectively, retaining the required sign and
50–150% magnitude range. Each familywise interval used 2,000 valid paired
60-second-block bootstrap replicates.

## Descriptive scale

- A 10 m greater H1 goalward offset was associated with -0.10161 m in the
  observed near-minus-middle response; across its pooled 10th–90th percentile
  span (31.94 m), the fitted difference was -0.32453 m.
- A 10 m greater H2 attacker–ball distance was associated with -0.07533 m;
  across its pooled 10th–90th percentile span (37.95 m), the fitted difference
  was -0.28586 m.

In plain football language, in these governed samples the measured localized
defender-relative difference was smaller, on average, when the focal attacker
started farther goalward of the defensive unit or farther from the ball,
conditional on the four frozen movement/depth controls. These are associations
in observed geometry, not evidence that either starting relationship caused a
defensive action or identifies a tactical reason for it.

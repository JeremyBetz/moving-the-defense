## Audit conclusion

The repository now has a current-facing narrative that matches its actual scientific position: the historical state ontology was challenged, the umbrella relational-reconfiguration design failed its Phase 3 validation test, and the next empirical object is the narrower focal-departure primitive under a frozen held-out protocol. Historical notebooks and the research log remain intact as an audit trail.

This is a well-documented research program, not yet a validated tactical metric or manuscript-ready empirical contribution.

## Terminology and mathematical presentation

- Current focal departure is defined once as $\mathbf r_d(t)=\mathbf x_d(t)-\mathbf c_{-d}(t)$, with the goalkeeper and focal defender excluded from the reference.
- Path, endpoint displacement, centroid motion, generic activity, pairwise closure, absolute approach, residual approach, and local deformation remain distinct quantities.
- “Focal departure” is geometric and continuous. It does not imply a departure from responsibility or tactical shape.
- “Relational reconfiguration” remains an unvalidated umbrella construct.
- “Defensive relational reallocation” is historical/provisional language, not a measured zero-sum transfer.
- “Engage” is provisional and does not establish opponent responsibility or intention.
- “Local deformation” is broader than compression; neither implies defensive success or failure.
- GitHub-facing equations use supported dollar-delimited math in current documentation.

## Current target in plain language

**Focal departure** is the extent to which one outfield defender’s movement differs from the contemporaneous collective motion of the remaining defending outfield players. Phase 4C externally replicated its geometric measurement behavior across seven professional matches from one independent tracking dataset/provider environment under frozen criteria. It remains activity-associated geometry—not automatically tactical, opponent-associated, opponent-induced, good, bad, or evidence of reconfiguration.

## Phase 4C evidence boundary

**Supported:** the focal-relative path is operationally computable in both Metrica and IDSSE; leave-one-out focal-versus-collective geometry is reproducible; primary distributional integrity and generic-activity relationship structure replicated across seven IDSSE matches; the contemporaneous reference consistently outperformed the frozen temporally misaligned control; common translation cancelled as mathematically expected; all frozen window/smoothing conclusions were robust; and the frozen external multi-match classification is **A**.

**Not supported by Phase 4C alone:** an activity-independent defensive adjustment, contextual expectation, tactical defensive response, relational reconfiguration, attacker-specific response, pinning, dragging, tracking, covering, handoffs, responsibility, attention, tactical intent, causal attacker influence, defensive quality, gravity, or off-ball attacking value. Phase 5A later supplies contextual-expectation feasibility evidence within IDSSE; it does not retroactively broaden Phase 4C.

Phase 4C's evidence stops at **individual/local behavior relative to collective movement**. Phase 5A separately adds feasibility evidence for contextual expectation. IDSSE adds one independent tracking dataset/provider environment relative to Metrica—not seven independent providers and not general provider invariance.

The post-validation umbrella is **defensive response**: observable defensive behavior occurring in the context of attacking positioning or movement. It is a future research target, not a validated construct. Relational reconfiguration is one possible intermediate response form and remains unvalidated.

Phase 5A's **contextual departure residual** is only observed focal-relative path minus model-predicted focal-relative path. Its diagnostic correlations with the named activity variables are small, but it is not established as activity-independent adjustment, tactical surprise, defensive response, attacker effect, or value.

Phase 5B v1.0 executed once and classified B — mixed/partial. Its selected attackers are deterministic geometric neighbors, not inferred assignments. The tested nearest-opponent representation does not provide evidence that local opponent information is materially more predictive than nonlocal opponent information. This constraint applies to the frozen representation rather than football universally. Tactical defensive-response interpretation and attacker attribution remain unvalidated.

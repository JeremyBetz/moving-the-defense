# Local Defensive Response Form — Prior Art and Provenance

**Audit date:** 2026-09-01

**Scope:** targeted method audit completed before any local-response-form coefficient, distribution, or match result. Inclusion records relevance, not endorsement or equivalence.

The governing separation is:

> **Football concept ≠ tracking measurement ≠ theoretical mechanism.**

The project already validates the *amount* of focal defender movement relative to the defensive unit and a two-match spatial footprint of its association with preceding attacker movement. The next protocol decomposes endpoint geometry without interpreting it as tracking, marking, attention, responsibility, influence, or causation.

## Design implications

1. Directional projection, radial projection, and collective-relative displacement have clear prior methodological neighbors. Their use is not claimed as mathematical novelty.
2. Relative phase, vector coding, velocity alignment, pressure, marking networks, learned defensive velocity, and pursuit-evasion models answer different questions and carry semantics that do not transfer automatically.
3. V1 therefore uses raw two-dimensional endpoint vectors and one transparent signed projection rather than a network, latent assignment, learned response, phase category, or tactical class.
4. The attacker axis is defined from the already-governed exposure interval; the radial axis is frozen at response start. Neither uses subsequent defender movement to select its reference.
5. Local teammate compensation is deferred. Existing network/coordination approaches demonstrate why it is interesting, but v1 has no defensible assignment-free teammate rule beyond the already-fixed rank regions.
